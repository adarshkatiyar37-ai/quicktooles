A workbench of small tools, not another app to manage

QuickTooles is a set of 56 focused utilities for the tasks that come up every day — formatting a messy JSON file, checking a word count, resizing an image, or working out an EMI. Open the one you need, use it, close the tab. Nothing to configure first.

Why people keep this tab open

🚀

Runs the moment you open it

Every tool works client-side in JavaScript, so there's no server round-trip and no loading screen between you and the result.

🔒

Your files stay on your device

Text, images and documents are processed locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a server unless the tool tells you otherwise.

💸

Free, with no catch

No sign-up, no trial period, no usage cap. Every tool stays free the next time you need it too.

Questions people ask

Are the tools on QuickTooles really free?

Yes. All 56 tools — image converters, code minifiers, calculators and the rest — are free with no hidden charges or subscriptions.

Do I need to install anything?

No. QuickTooles runs entirely in your browser on any device — phone, tablet or desktop. There's nothing to download.

Is my data safe when I use the text or image tools?

Yes. Most tools process your text, images and data locally on your device — we don't store or track your input.
